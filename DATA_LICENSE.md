# Data attribution

The numerical datasets in `data/` are distributed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- **Airfoil Self-Noise:** Thomas Brooks, D. Pope, and Michael Marcolini (1989), UCI Machine Learning Repository, [doi:10.24432/C5VW2C](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5VW2C). The 1,503 rows contain frequency, angle of attack, chord length, free-stream velocity, suction-side displacement thickness, and scaled sound pressure level, in that order.
- **Concrete Compressive Strength:** I-Cheng Yeh (1998), UCI Machine Learning Repository, [doi:10.24432/C5PK67](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5PK67). The 1,030 rows contain cement, blast-furnace slag, fly ash, water, superplasticizer, coarse aggregate, fine aggregate, age, and compressive strength, in that order.

Both files are numeric CSV exports with original values, row order, and column order preserved. The final column is the response. Download locations and checksums are recorded in `data/provenance.json`.

The experiment software is distributed under the MIT license in `LICENSE`.
